The Hi-Lo system

Every card you see is worth +1, 0, or −1. Keep a running total in your head as the shoe empties — that's your running count.

2 · 3 · 4 · 5 · 6+1
7 · 8 · 90
10 · J · Q · K · A−1
Running count is the raw sum of every card seen since the last shuffle.

True count = running count ÷ decks remaining. With more than one deck in play, the true count is what actually tells you the odds — a +6 running count means very little in an 8-deck shoe, but a lot in a single deck.
Why hide it?

A real table never shows you the count. CountJack hides it too — tap Check Count whenever you want to test yourself. Guess first, then see how close you were.
